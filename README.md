## Helpful Tips and Tricks

* Work with adjustment layers and masks to keep edits reversible and clean.
* Name and group layers early to stay organized in large compositions.
* Use smart objects before transforming or filtering to preserve image quality.

* Save custom brushes, gradients, and workspaces to reuse favorite looks.


## Complete Overview of Adobe Photoshop

Adobe Photoshop is a professional raster graphics editor for photo retouching, compositing, digital painting, and graphic design. It centers on layered editing, precise color management, and broad file format support, from RAW photos to web and print exports. Non-destructive tools such as smart objects, adjustment layers, and masks allow experimentation without harming original pixels.

It fits photographers, designers, illustrators, marketers, and content creators who need polished visuals. Beginners can rely on presets, guided actions, and a streamlined workspace, while advanced users can work with channels, advanced masking, automation, color profiles, and motion-ready workflows for commercial projects.

Choosing an edition depends on your focus and team size. A single-app option suits dedicated photo and design work, a photography bundle suits those who also manage large photo libraries, and a full creative suite suits teams moving between photo, video, illustration, and layout tasks. Compare the simplified options below to find the best match for your goals.

## Quick Start Walkthrough

1. Open Adobe Photoshop and create a new document with your preferred size and resolution.
2. Bring in photos through File > Open or File > Place Embedded for layered work.
3. Review the Layers, Masks, and Adjustments panels to plan your edit.
4. Apply color adjustments, retouching brushes, and smart filters as needed.
5. Save a master copy as PSD to preserve layers for future changes.
6. Export a final copy as PNG, JPG, or TIFF for print or online sharing.
